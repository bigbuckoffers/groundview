Order Terms & Conditions

By placing an order you agree to pay for services according to our service agreement. If you are not satisfied with the photos received, you have 7 days from delivery to open a dispute through your message center. After 7 days, the order is considered completed to your satisfaction and the transaction is final.

Once a field agent accepts your order, it cannot be canceled. Standard orders placed before 10:00 AM in the property's local time zone are fulfilled by the next business day (Monday–Friday, excluding holidays). Specific delivery times are not guaranteed. Inclement weather may cause delays — we will never put our field agents in unsafe situations.

For safety and legal reasons, all exterior photography is taken from public property only. Our field agents will not enter private property or private roads for exterior orders.

If a resident or occupant becomes hostile, the field agent will reschedule. If threatened, the order will be canceled immediately for their safety.

Important — Multi-Unit Properties: By placing this order, you confirm this is the property type selected above. If our field agent arrives and finds additional units not accounted for, a $20 surcharge per additional unit will be applied automatically.

A $0.50 processing fee applies to any canceled order to cover mapping and third-party costs. This is non-refundable.
